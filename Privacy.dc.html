What is collected

Nothing you do on this site is logged, profiled, or tied to an identity by this site. There are no accounts, no forms that store submissions, no site cookies, and no local storage of personal data.

The web host that serves these files may keep standard server access logs (IP address, timestamp, requested file, user agent) for security and uptime purposes. Those logs are retained by the host under its own policy and are not used by me for analytics or profiling.

Third-party services

Google Fonts
Typefaces are requested from Google’s font CDN, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
Outbound links
Links to Google Scholar, ORCID, GitHub, LinkedIn, publisher sites, and the USPTO take you to third parties governed by their own privacy policies.
Privacy-enhanced YouTube embeds
When a published video is played, it uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com). Google/YouTube may process data or set cookies under its own privacy policy when the player loads or is used. This site does not embed comment widgets, social buttons, or tag managers.

Email & correspondence

If you email the address listed on this site, your message and address are used only to reply to you and are kept in my university mailbox. They are never added to a mailing list, sold, or shared with a third party.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected here, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete. If you have corresponded with me by email and want that correspondence deleted, ask and I will delete it.

Visitors covered by the GDPR or CCPA may exercise those rights against the same address; there is no data broker relationship and no sale of personal information to opt out of.
